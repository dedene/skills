# 22 — Copy: messaging, formulas, storytelling, specificity, length, QA

"The words sell your offers; the website just needs to properly serve that content" `[W:ClD037qJSTQ 2025]`. Sales copy is "more of a science than an art": specific elements in a specific order `[W:jPclFEiC2o4 2023]`. Caveat from 2026: when AI makes every site well written, good copy stops being the tiebreaker, so differentiation must come from specifics, proof and human presence (C19) `[W:VXGDHZIGf40 2026]`.

## Messaging rules

**COPY-01 — Write in the customer's words, not the industry's.** The "curse of knowledge": experts describe their work differently from clients `[W:-n392pJo00I 2024]`. Audit: count the jargon on your homepage ("streamline solutions", "optimize your workflow", "leverage your expertise"), terms you'd never say out loud `[W:rByridDOAKM 2025]`. Replace with what prospects say at night ("I'm drowning in admin work") `[W:rByridDOAKM 2025]`. Mechanism: language-style matching; reflecting their words back makes them think "this person gets me" `[W:aKUoWPGph-M 2024]`. Use the phrases consistently everywhere (homepage, services, About, ads, email subject lines); don't slip back to old language after one use `[W:rByridDOAKM 2025]`.

**COPY-02 — Customer is the hero, you are the mentor.** Talk about them; they "see themselves reflected". A business talking about itself + a client who wants to talk about themselves = impasse `[W:hDgjahAAi0g 2023]`. Mentors "enter from a position of strength": they've been there, they understand, they support rather than brag. Luke/Yoda, Marty/Doc `[W:bWVyN9VoBB4 2026]`. Test: the **French test** (count "we/our/us" vs "you/your"); every page except About should pass with "you" dominant `[W:att9LfaZBos 2023]`.

**COPY-03 — Turn every brag into a client benefit.** "I'm the best wedding DJ in town" → "Your dance floor will feel like the center of your universe… your friends and family will repeat 'that was the best wedding I've ever been to' until you're almost sick of hearing it" `[W:bYZkDOwQxfg 2022]`. `[MS]` "So what?" test: after every claim ask "so what?"; if the copy doesn't answer, add "which means…".

**COPY-04 — Benefits first, features to justify.** Emotion decides, logic justifies (Wes repeats "95% of decisions are emotional"; illustrative) `[W:hDgjahAAi0g 2023, kZdXTq5uOEI 2021]`. Formula: **"We offer [feature] so you can [benefit]"** `[W:b0KQaZkDBKU 2018]`, or as a card: **1–3-word benefit title + one sentence naming the feature behind it** ("Up close encounters — get closer to the whales than ever with our custom charter options") `[W:1kgolxbvCd8 ≤2024]`. Go one level deeper to the emotional payoff: dog food "fresh whole-food diet" → "help dogs live up to 2½ years longer" → "more mealtimes together" `[W:mGbLhEY7nPE 2024]`. Features don't disappear; they move lower for the logical checkers `[W:hDgjahAAi0g 2023]`. Mine benefits from features competitors lack or don't talk about `[W:b0KQaZkDBKU 2018]`.

**COPY-05 — Aim at self-image and social image.** Two emotions recur in purchases: how they want to feel about themselves after, and how they want others to see them `[W:OtChoVhEI8Y 2025]`.

**COPY-06 — Repeat the one belief that must be true (truth effect).** Decide the single thing prospects must believe before they'll book (a USP, "strategy matters", a guarantee) and repeat it in headlines, lists, videos and chosen testimonials "like a mantra". "If you think you're saying the same thing too many times, probably say it once or twice more." Side effect: it filters out bad-fit leads `[W:dKn4Zxpu9jk 2018, IH6wsGmgi8s 2023]`.

**COPY-07 — Use loss framing sparingly and truthfully.** People fear losing more than they enjoy gaining: say what they give up by doing nothing or choosing wrong (accountant: deductions missed doing taxes yourself); "Stop wasting money" over "Start saving money" `[W:dKn4Zxpu9jk 2018]`. Agitate with empathy, not fear-mongering `[W:OtChoVhEI8Y 2025]`.

**COPY-08 — Conversational, human, plain.** Write the way you'd explain it over coffee; contractions; drop term-paper grammar; slang only if your customers use it; humor only if it fits and you can pull it off `[W:fyprFI44rN0 2023]`. Casual tone for every industry, from plumbers to lawyers `[W:jPclFEiC2o4 2023]`. Simple language signals mastery (Einstein: "If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it well enough") `[W:2ncy12qlabw 2023]`. Example: "Low visibility is keeping your product out of reach" → "People who need your product can't find it" `[W:mGbLhEY7nPE 2024]`. Big brands (LegalZoom vs a local attorney) are more human than small firms that "bark louder" with jargon to look legit `[W:fyprFI44rN0 2023]`.

**COPY-09 — Reading level grade 7–8.** Grade 7–8 (US) ≈ CEFR B1 / "klare taal" (NL) / "langage clair" (FR). Aim for a 7th-grade level even for smart audiences; "using simple language isn't dumbing it down; it's removing the friction" `[W:IQtpeWeVqt0 2026]`. Earlier guidance: 8th grade, even for savvy B2B `[W:dUrlwvm-CqQ 2024, t4gF4aVRcDY 2024]`. One core idea per section `[W:IQtpeWeVqt0 2026]` `[MS]`. For complex ideas, use one fitting metaphor (anxiety avoidance = "holding a beach ball underwater") `[W:IQtpeWeVqt0 2026]`.

**COPY-10 — Say what you are, early.** One agency didn't mention being a marketing agency until the third section `[W:2lYdI86YV30 2023]`. The opposite failure also exists: too little information, assuming the problem is obvious or the service "sells itself" `[W:g_Wk6nJD1_Y 2025]`.

**COPY-11 — Be specific; kill clichés.** Precision creates a "zoom-in" effect that catches skimmers; clichés are ignored by scanners and eye-rolled by readers `[W:RlMBBbXzQ84 2023]`. Clichés creep in exactly where you try to explain what makes you unique.

| Cliché | Specific rewrite |
|---|---|
| "Customer satisfaction is our top priority" | "We guarantee a 1-hour response time on every support request, so your issue is solved fast" |
| "We go the extra mile" (caterer) | "We work closely with you to tailor the menu to your guests' dietary preferences and restrictions" |
| "We're the leading provider" (agency) | "Known for projects that grow clients' website visitors by 35% on average within 3 months" |
| "Spend more, earn more" | "Get up to 3% Daily Cash back on every purchase" (Apple Card) |
| "We'll pack your dance floor" (DJ) | "Making you dance till your feet bleed since 2005" `[W:fyprFI44rN0 2023]` |
| "Tailored to your unique needs" | Describe the actual process; the cliché signals "I'll wing it" `[W:g_Wk6nJD1_Y 2025]` |
| "Your choice for…", "preferred provider" | A benefit: how the event goes and how you get them there `[W:7hhQf3T3k-Q 2019]` |

Rows 1–4: `[W:RlMBBbXzQ84 2023]`. Ban test: if a dry cleaner or dog trainer could use the tagline, rewrite it `[W:7kG8otQJfws 2021]`. "That's not a slogan, that's an actual promise."

**COPY-12 — Numbers: exact, unrounded, sourced.** Numbers raise credibility; unrounded numbers feel researched ("precision bias") `[W:RlMBBbXzQ84 2023]`. See `PROOF-10`.

**COPY-13 — Show, don't summarize.** One or two concrete details beat labels: "he kept asking me, 'What happens if I miss a whole week?'" instead of "he was worried"; "It was 6:00 a.m., I'd been staring at a blank screen for 40 minutes and my coffee had gone cold" instead of "a rough morning" `[W:bWVyN9VoBB4 2026]`.

**COPY-14 — Zoom into recognizable moments.** Personal trainer: excited in January, going less in February, stopped by March, still paying for the membership in April. "You're showing them them on a page" `[W:OtChoVhEI8Y 2025]`.

**COPY-15 — The "I know you" move.** "I know you feel [X]. I know you've tried [Y]. Here's why it never worked for you, and here's what will." `[W:OtChoVhEI8Y 2025]`.

**COPY-16 — Emotional buy-in is not manipulation.** Focus on what the service means for their life. Limo company: "arriving at your big event in style" before makes, models and hours `[W:kZdXTq5uOEI 2021]`.

**COPY-17 — Use "how I", not "how to", in content and About.** Your own experience meets less resistance and "no one can question you" on it `[W:oy6gte9oWVo 2024]`. Admitting a past mistake raises trust ("I used to recommend this until it backfired on three clients") `[W:IQtpeWeVqt0 2026]`.

## Headline formulas beyond the hero

Hero formulas: the `page-copy` skill's `hero.md` (HERO-02). Section and page headlines:

- **Section headings as mini-headlines** (`STRUCT-06`): "[Benefit/feeling] + [service]": "Why people love our dog-sitting service" `[W:RlMBBbXzQ84 2023]`.
- **Problem section:** "Does this sound familiar?" / a yes-question naming the pain `[W:Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025, OtChoVhEI8Y 2025]` / "[Frustration 1], [frustration 2]? You're not alone, and it's not your fault." `[W:QJnhnhXuuYM 2025]` / "When you're at the office, worry still tags along" `[W:trfDoGoXNsE 2025]`.
- **Solution section:** "It doesn't need to be this way" + the promise `[W:jsOoglMSpdY 2024]`; "We keep your dog moving and your mind at peace" `[W:trfDoGoXNsE 2025]`.
- **Testimonials:** "Look what our patients are doing now" `[W:7kG8otQJfws 2021]`; "They did it, so can you" `[W:Yd8HdycDiHI 2018]`.
- **Final CTA:** "Are you ready to [outcome]?" / "Ready for worry-free walks that fit your schedule?" `[W:Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025, trfDoGoXNsE 2025]`.
- **About:** templates in the `page-copy` skill's `page-types.md`.
- **Adapted `[MS]` formulas (use when they fit SMB copy):** "[Outcome] without [pain]" ("Spotless windows without the ladder"); "Never [unpleasant event] again" (Wes's "Never wax your car again"); "Stop [pain]. Start [pleasure]." (Wes's "Stop people pleasing, start living true to you"); "[Outcome] in [timeframe]"; "The [category] for [specific audience]"; "The [category] that [differentiator]".
- **"Now you can…" test `[MS]`:** prefix a headline or benefit with "Now you can…". If the result is compelling *and* true, keep it; feature-speak ("Now you can have a powerful platform") collapses.

## Storytelling

**STORY-01 — On the website, story = structure, not narrative.** Lead visitors through "I have this problem → this person can help me → I'm ready for the next step", named the "customer saga" `[W:bWVyN9VoBB4 2026, 2lYdI86YV30 2023]`. Frameworks per section: `STRUCT-04`.

**STORY-02 — What storytelling is not:** third-person tales ("Meet Sally, who struggled with bookkeeping…"), your company origin story on sales pages, creative-writing fluff, autobiographies instead of customer focus `[W:OtChoVhEI8Y 2025]`. Origin stories go to About or nowhere `[W:7hhQf3T3k-Q 2019, 2lYdI86YV30 2023]`.

**STORY-03 — Micro-stories (content, testimonials, case snippets): five beats** — Situation, Desire, Conflict, Change, Result. Present tense to relive the moment; share your real inner thought ("Honestly, my first thought was this guy's going to quit on me within a month"); bridge back with "The reason I'm telling you this is…" `[W:bWVyN9VoBB4 2026]`. Keep them ordinary but relatable, not dramatic.

**STORY-04 — Stories by funnel stage:** micro-stories in content (top), customer-saga structure on the website (middle), story → point → bridge in email (bottom) `[W:bWVyN9VoBB4 2026]`. Email version in `lead-offer.md`.

**STORY-05 — Story sources:** daily life (what frustrated you, what you learned the hard way), client breakthroughs and struggles (change names), the outside world (articles, history). Work backwards from the point you want to make `[W:bWVyN9VoBB4 2026]`.

## Copy QA sweep (run in order; loop back after edits)

Adapted from the `[MS]` Seven Sweeps, with Wes's tests plugged in. Each sweep looks for one thing only. After fixing a sweep, re-check the earlier ones.

1. **Clarity.** 5-second test on the hero (HERO-01). An outsider can say what you do. No jargon, no unexplained role terms ("birth nurse") or framework names in the hero `[W:nfFlB0pmsWc 2020]`. One idea per section. Nothing mysterious or unresolved.
2. **Customer language and voice.** Pain and outcome phrases trace to real customer quotes (brief). French test passes (except About). Tone consistent (no casual-then-corporate drift) `[MS]`. Formality matches the locale.
3. **So what.** Every feature has a "which means…" bridge to a benefit; every benefit reaches a feeling or life outcome `[MS]` `[W:mGbLhEY7nPE 2024]`.
4. **Prove it.** Every claim has proof within one scroll: a testimonial that says the same thing, a number, a logo, a guarantee `[MS]` `[W:mGbLhEY7nPE 2024]`. Unprovable superlatives are cut or softened. No invented stats or testimonials.
5. **Specificity.** Replace vague words with numbers, timeframes, examples; unrounded numbers; kill every cliché from the list; if a line can't be made specific, it's probably filler `[MS]` `[W:RlMBBbXzQ84 2023]`.
6. **Emotion.** Before-state is felt (a zoomed-in moment), after-state is visible (self-image, social image), agitation stays empathetic `[W:OtChoVhEI8Y 2025]` `[MS]`.
7. **Zero risk.** Near every CTA: what happens next, how long, what it costs, the booster line, the answer to the top objection, price information or a link to it, any guarantee `[MS]` `[W:mGbLhEY7nPE 2024]`.
8. **Consistency (Wes add-on).** One CTA wording everywhere; process Step 1 uses the CTA wording; the "mantra" belief appears in hero, a benefit, a testimonial and the FAQ `[W:Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025, dKn4Zxpu9jk 2018]`.
9. **Locale (BE add-on, [G]).** Belgian Dutch (not Netherlands Dutch) or Belgian French usage, correct formality, local place names, VAT-inclusive consumer prices (`be-eu.md`). Hand NL copy to the `belgisch-nederlands-redacteur` skill.

Final checks: typos, links, phone number format, no leftover template text, bolded AI changes reviewed. When AI drafts copy, have it bold all new text so every change gets human review `[W:aKUoWPGph-M 2024, -n392pJo00I 2024]`.

## AI-assisted drafting notes

- Don't prompt "write a homepage for a plumber in Dallas": AI doesn't know the conversion flow. Decide the sections first, then prompt section by section `[W:1kgolxbvCd8 ≤2024]`.
- Prompt anatomy: context first ("the part so many people miss"), then role, task, output format, audience; repeat audience details in each prompt `[W:-n392pJo00I 2024]`.
- Priority when rewriting a page: customer messaging > CRO > SEO ("SEO content can sometimes get in the way") `[W:-n392pJo00I 2024]`.
- Over-generate and whittle down (e.g. 10 benefit-feature combos → pick 3) `[W:1kgolxbvCd8 ≤2024]`.
- AI output is a starting point: it "sounded exactly like AI, like a term paper" until tone was configured; always do a voice and accuracy pass `[W:1kgolxbvCd8 ≤2024, trfDoGoXNsE 2025]`. Run final prose through the `humanizer` / `stop-slop` skills [G].
