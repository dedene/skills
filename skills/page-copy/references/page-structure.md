# 21 — Page structure, navigation, scannability, visual design, mobile

Wes's structural thesis: the homepage is a **fixed path**, like IKEA's showroom, where each section builds on the last, and "the order of sections matters just as much" as which sections you include `[W:jsOoglMSpdY 2024]`. "Think of it like a combination lock" `[W:Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025]`. On the site you don't *tell* a narrative; you lead visitors through a story-like structure: "I have this problem" → "this person can help me" → "I'm ready for the next step" `[W:bWVyN9VoBB4 2026]`.

## STRUCT-01 — Decide the homepage type from the offer mix

| Offer mix | Homepage type | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| One offer ≥ ~80% of revenue, or a single-offer business | **Landing page for that offer** | Full blueprint below about that offer; other services as add-ons with their own page `[W:_hBEZT8-uOw 2018, 7hhQf3T3k-Q 2019, b0KQaZkDBKU 2018]` |
| One flagship, others secondary | **Flagship-weighted** | Blueprint for the flagship; services section gives the others smaller cards `[W:_hBEZT8-uOw 2018]` |
| Equal offers for different buyers (buy vs sell; residential vs commercial) | **Router** ("pick a lane") | One headline covering both + two clear routes to dedicated pages that do the selling `[W:_hBEZT8-uOw 2018]` |
| Visitors can't tell which package they need | **Blueprint + package finder** | Quiz recommends the package, email-gated result `[W:2lYdI86YV30 2023]` |

Don't lump services a buyer wouldn't expect from one vendor into one headline ("I wouldn't think I would go to the same vendor for both") `[W:7hhQf3T3k-Q 2019]`. Equal emphasis on everything makes you look too general and turns off the bread-and-butter majority `[W:_hBEZT8-uOw 2018]`.

## STRUCT-02 — The homepage blueprint (canonical order)

The 2025 ten-section blueprint `[W:Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025]`, consistent with the 2023 "Customer Saga" (problem → solution → benefits → how it works → testimonials) `[W:2lYdI86YV30 2023]` and the 2024 IKEA formula `[W:jsOoglMSpdY 2024]`.

| # | Section | Job | Content rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hero** | What, why it matters, what next, why you | `hero.md` |
| 2 | **Problem** | Show you understand their situation better than they can describe it | "Does this sound familiar?" + 3–5 symptom bullets in their words; include what they've tried that didn't work. Empathy, not fear-mongering. Optional image of the pain (a sad dog waiting by the door) `[W:Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025, trfDoGoXNsE 2025, OtChoVhEI8Y 2025]`. "If you can talk about their problem better than they can, they'll trust you know how to fix it" `[W:bWVyN9VoBB4 2026]` |
| 3 | **Solution / guide** | First appearance of you, as mentor | "It doesn't need to be this way" + what you saw broken in the market + **empathy and authority** (both; empathy without authority "falls on deaf ears", authority without empathy doesn't connect). First real photo of you/team. Short. Optional stat row (clients, years) `[W:Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025, jsOoglMSpdY 2024, trfDoGoXNsE 2025]` |
| 4 | **Services** (skip if single offer) | Shortcuts for people who already know what they want | 3–5 cards (the ~80%-of-revenue services), each linking to its own page `[W:Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025, jsOoglMSpdY 2024, 7kG8otQJfws 2021]` |
| 5 | **Benefits** | Paint the after picture ("this is how good life could look if I choose you") | Exactly 3; benefit title (1–3 words) + one sentence naming the enabling feature and the deeper meaning; icon or photo each `[W:Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025, _hBEZT8-uOw 2018]` |
| 6 | **Process** | Lower the fear of starting; set expectations | Exactly 3 steps even if the real process has 20. Step 1 = same words as the CTA + its benefit; Step 2 = everything condensed ("we talk, we plan, we build"); Step 3 = the end result they enjoy ("a cheat, not so much a step") `[W:Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025]`. "A confused mind always says no" `[W:jsOoglMSpdY 2024]` |
| 7 | **Testimonials** | Show others made the journey | 1 featured (ideally video, with its best line as text) + 3 short ones; each headlined with its result; photo + five stars; show the crowd, not just a few quotes `[W:Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025, jsOoglMSpdY 2024]` |
| 8 | **Features / what's included** (optional comparison) | Let logical buyers justify the emotional yes | Checklist of inclusions (≤ ~10 bullets, checkmarks); optional balanced comparison vs alternatives `[W:Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025, _hBEZT8-uOw 2018, b0KQaZkDBKU 2018]` |
| 9 | **FAQ** | Remove last objections | ~6 on the homepage; every other one an objection rephrased as a question with your best rebuttal ("Why can't I go with someone cheaper?") `[W:Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025]` |
| 10 | **Final CTA** | Catch the decided | Question headline ("Ready for worry-free walks that fit your schedule?") + same button as the hero; as a TL;DR panel: offer + main benefit + one reason + button `[W:Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025, trfDoGoXNsE 2025, mGbLhEY7nPE 2024]` |
| — | Footer | Secondary pages, trust, legal | Phone; no raw email as main contact (but see BE legal); secondary nav; credibility logos ("where people look to contact you") `[W:3ahiFlIHvDc 2022, trfDoGoXNsE 2025]` |

**Optional inserts:** "Wild card" section after benefits or process, dedicated to the #1 objection, using stats, your unique approach, or a mini case study `[W:hDgjahAAi0g 2023]`. Transitional-offer section (lead magnet with its own form) between testimonials and FAQ `[W:_hBEZT8-uOw 2018]`. Differentiator comparison ("standard appraisal vs ours") `[W:7kG8otQJfws 2021]`. Visual businesses: "meet the fleet / see the space" lower down, never as the hero `[W:7kG8otQJfws 2021]`. SEO copy block at the very bottom, designed to look premium `[W:7kG8otQJfws 2021]`.

**Allowed deviations (C17):** emergency/utility trades may put benefits and proof straight after the hero (the problem is obvious and urgent). Warm-traffic sites may merge problem and solution into one before-after-bridge section. Never move the hero, and never end without a CTA section.

## STRUCT-03 — Landing page orders

**Paid landing page (cold, one offer).** Never send ads to the homepage ("way too general") `[W:kZdXTq5uOEI 2021]`. Advertise an **offer**, not the brand: free intro service, lead magnet, discount, value-add; each offer gets its own page `[W:kZdXTq5uOEI 2021]`.

1. Eyebrow echoing the ad's audience/promise `[W:mGbLhEY7nPE 2024]`, headline matching the ad copy `[MS]`
2. Hero: result headline, how subhead, one CTA, booster, proof row above the fold
3. Problem (PAS) or before-after-bridge
4. 3 benefits (emotional first), each followed by the testimonial that proves it `[W:mGbLhEY7nPE 2024]`
5. ~60 s video: problem, how you solve it, how life improves, what is lost by choosing another option or nothing; ends with the same CTA `[W:b0KQaZkDBKU 2018]`
6. 3–5 short objection-busting testimonials **on the page itself**, not a link to a testimonials page `[W:kZdXTq5uOEI 2021]`
7. Features checklist (checkmarks) for logical justification `[W:b0KQaZkDBKU 2018]`
8. Balanced comparison (incl. "doing nothing") `[W:mGbLhEY7nPE 2024]`
9. FAQ / risk reversal
10. TL;DR CTA panel `[W:mGbLhEY7nPE 2024]`

Rules: no navigation, no links out, no company history or charity work `[W:b0KQaZkDBKU 2018]` `[MS]`. **Visual continuity with the ad** (colors, fonts, image style; not necessarily the same image), because a mismatch makes people think they clicked wrong "and guess what, you just paid for that click" (matters most for image ads on social) `[W:kZdXTq5uOEI 2021]`. Same offer as promised in the ad `[MS]`. Fast load (`STRUCT-13`).

**Organic landing / service page (SEO).** "Service pages are home pages": many visitors land there straight from Google, so give them the same persuasive elements and order as the homepage, not an SEO wall of text `[W:hDgjahAAi0g 2023]`. Keep nav (visitors need to explore). Also receiving ad clicks? Apply the hybrid rule in C2 (`principles.md`). Keyword + city in the subhead, specific section headings, SEO block at the bottom. Wireframe in `page-types.md`.

**Pre-sell article (advertorial).** For prospects who don't yet believe they need a pro: an article that educates (real tips), makes the gap between amateur DIY and a pro result visible, scatters photos of your finished work, then ends with sales-page elements (benefits → testimonial row → CTA form). Run it **alongside** direct-to-offer ads, not instead ("scaling up by scaling out" across awareness levels) `[W:WktIWzpeMis 2020]`. The title does as much work as everything else combined `[W:WktIWzpeMis 2020]`.

**Capture page for warm video traffic.** When 10 minutes of video already built trust: headline promising the direct result + email-only field + button. Nothing else `[W:lxndWdjOyVM 2026]`.

## STRUCT-04 — Story frameworks mapped to sections

From Talia Wolf via Wes `[W:OtChoVhEI8Y 2025]`. Storytelling on a page means "making it about the customer" in the second person, showing their transformation. It is **not** third-person narratives ("Meet Sally…", cheesy), not your origin story, not creative-writing fluff.

| Framework | Fits | Sections |
|---|---|---|
| **PAS** | Most homepages and LPs | Problem (a yes-question: "Have you lost touch with what's most important?") → Agitate (symptoms, to show you get it) → Solution (you + a unique qualification). Three sections |
| **Before–After–Bridge** | One tight section; warm traffic | Current situation → the after → your offer as the bridge |
| **Hero's Journey** | Long-form pages | Set the stage (their world) → call to adventure (pain) → meet the mentor (you: empathy + experience + a selectivity stat) → overcome obstacles (3-step process) → return with the elixir (the transformation) |

## STRUCT-05 — Navigation

- **≤ 5 items**, only pages that help a visitor become a customer (Services, About, Pricing, Testimonials/Reviews, FAQ); everything else (Blog, Jobs, Media, Client login) goes to the footer `[W:3ahiFlIHvDc 2022, dUrlwvm-CqQ 2024, pRNny_t8TUE 2025]`. Wes: five is "the magic number" (experience-based heuristic). No two-row navs, heavy dropdowns or mega menus. *Why:* paradox of choice (jam study, illustrative; choice-overload effects are weaker in replications [G]) and Hick's law `[W:IH6wsGmgi8s 2023, pRNny_t8TUE 2025]`.
- **CTA button in the top right**, styled as the button (not a nav link that blends in) `[W:7hhQf3T3k-Q 2019, 2lYdI86YV30 2023]`. The top-right "place of honor" is for the CTA, not a client login `[W:7hhQf3T3k-Q 2019]`. Sticky header with the CTA is fine `[W:_hBEZT8-uOw 2018]`.
- Always-visible nav lets different visitors jump to what they care about (pricing, testimonials, FAQ) while the page still tells a linear story `[W:_hBEZT8-uOw 2018]`.
- **No social icons or feeds** in the header or homepage body: social is top of funnel; sending site visitors back there trades a customer for a follower `[W:dUrlwvm-CqQ 2024]`. Footer icons only for platforms you actually use `[W:trfDoGoXNsE 2025]`.
- Keep a site to "a handful of key pages" rather than a 30–50 page brochure where visitors wander and "come up with their own story" `[W:jPclFEiC2o4 2023, 8ZX9y8GP0xs 2022]`.
- Paid LPs: no nav (C2).

## STRUCT-06 — Scannability kit

People scan: "80 to 90 percent" skim the homepage; people read word for word only ~16% of the time (illustrative) `[W:_hBEZT8-uOw 2018, RlMBBbXzQ84 2023]`. "People don't read websites, they skim them" `[W:2lYdI86YV30 2023]`.

- A headline at every important point; **specific section headings, not labels**: "Testimonials" → "Why people love our dog-sitting service"; "About us" → "Why we're obsessed with keeping your pet healthy" (bonus: keywords in headings) `[W:RlMBBbXzQ84 2023]`.
- Paragraphs of 2–3 sentences max `[W:fyprFI44rN0 2023]`; sentences mostly under ~25 words `[MS]`.
- Bullet lists; checkmarks for inclusions (things they "check off") `[W:b0KQaZkDBKU 2018]`.
- **Bold the three ideas** you want a skimmer to take away ("if someone only reads three key ideas here, what do I want them to take away?") `[W:w79jVvGlIDE 2023]`.
- Long-form: enlarge the first sentence or paragraph and spend it on the most compelling pain (in one study 95% viewed all or part of a bigger opening paragraph; illustrative) `[W:w79jVvGlIDE 2023]`.
- Pull the best line of a long testimonial into a headline `[W:2ncy12qlabw 2023]`.
- Order your points so the piece feels like it improves: 2nd-best first, best second, 3rd-best last, the rest in between (from his video-script advice; apply to benefit lists and FAQs as a test) `[W:IQtpeWeVqt0 2026]`.

## STRUCT-07 — Simplicity and prototypicality

**Use the category's familiar layout; be creative inside it.** Google research (Tuch et al. 2012): people judge a site's beauty in ~1/50th of a second; visually complex sites were rated less beautiful; highly prototypical + simple sites were rated most beautiful. Attractiveness → credibility → trust `[W:dvmMn1J-kqk 2023]`. Working memory holds ~5–9 chunks; a familiar layout frees it for your benefits, UVP, guarantees and testimonials instead of decoding the page `[W:dvmMn1J-kqk 2023]`. The service-business template: wide hero, three-column benefits, services showcase, simple nav `[W:dvmMn1J-kqk 2023, pRNny_t8TUE 2025]`.

- Express creativity through color, fonts, spacing and imagery drawn from your real differentiators, and through small interactive features (quiz, calculator), never through structure or navigation `[W:dvmMn1J-kqk 2023]`.
- **"Good-enough design"**: not 1995, but it matches the mental template and gets out of the way. "It's not about how beautiful your site is. It's about whether it converts." `[W:pRNny_t8TUE 2025]`.
- Resist filling empty space: owners and designers add copy and images because "it feels like something's missing", which raises complexity and turns a client-generating tool into "an expensive art project" `[W:dvmMn1J-kqk 2023, pRNny_t8TUE 2025]`. Crestodina: high-converting sites had ~40% fewer page elements (illustrative).
- Templates are fine: "nobody's hiring you based on the uniqueness and creativity of your website" (unless you sell web design) `[W:ClD037qJSTQ 2025]`. One base template can look very different via images, colors and fonts `[W:2lYdI86YV30 2023]`.
- Krug: good design becomes invisible; Spool: "the moment someone notices your design is often the moment you failed" `[W:dvmMn1J-kqk 2023, pRNny_t8TUE 2025]`.

## STRUCT-08 — Visual design rules

- **Spacing:** at least ~80 px padding inside sections, breathing room around buttons; too much space is also bad `[W:8ZX9y8GP0xs 2022]`. White space "doesn't look empty, it just looks clean" `[W:fyprFI44rN0 2023]`.
- **Color:** neutral backgrounds; color as accent for buttons, headlines, icons; "use them to accentuate, not to dominate" `[W:dUrlwvm-CqQ 2024]`. Use a preset palette so non-designers don't make "a circus" `[W:trfDoGoXNsE 2025]`. The CTA color is reserved for CTAs (`CTA-05`). Subdued/neutral palettes read more premium (study of 78 sites; illustrative) `[W:RiNJ-rl4MSU 2022]`. The color should fit the buyer's world and price level ("IT-provider blue" doesn't say glamorous wedding) `[W:7hhQf3T3k-Q 2019]`. Refine an aggressive palette rather than throwing the brand away `[W:g_Wk6nJD1_Y 2025]`.
- **"Tight" design:** everything on a grid; off-kilter or homemade reads cheap, and cheap reads untrustworthy (halo effect) `[W:RiNJ-rl4MSU 2022]`. In low-trust industries (roofing, construction) an amateur site "confirms their fears about choosing the wrong contractor" `[W:g_Wk6nJD1_Y 2025]`.
- **Dark backgrounds** with light text are acceptable now (dark-mode habits); a judgment call per brand `[W:2lYdI86YV30 2023]`. A dark closing CTA band with white text is a standard move `[W:trfDoGoXNsE 2025]`.
- **Typography:** one site-wide headline + body pairing `[W:trfDoGoXNsE 2025]`.
- **Icons** from one pack only `[W:8ZX9y8GP0xs 2022]`.
- **Visual hierarchy:** the eye should flow headline → CTA → proof; when everything competes, good proof gets lost `[W:g_Wk6nJD1_Y 2025]`.
- **Variety inside convention:** alternate image sides, one brand-colored band (e.g. benefits) for impact, don't make every section "image on the right" `[W:trfDoGoXNsE 2025]` (C20).
- **Section height:** tighten over-tall sections; delete leftover template sections `[W:trfDoGoXNsE 2025]`.

## STRUCT-09 — Images across the page

- Purpose test: every image shows either the service result or happy customers `[W:pRNny_t8TUE 2025]`.
- Customer-story images throughout ("that could be me if I work with them"); keep the spotlight on customers, team photos on About and the solution section `[W:fyprFI44rN0 2023]`.
- **Real** for team, premises and portfolio; never stock or AI for the team ("feels like hiding") `[W:dUrlwvm-CqQ 2024, trfDoGoXNsE 2025, bYZkDOwQxfg 2022]`. Team snapshots needn't be a pro shoot, but not homemade: a modern phone in portrait mode, heads not cropped `[W:OvQvDu5ZKzQ 2020, trfDoGoXNsE 2025]`.
- Consistent image family: same vibrancy, energy and filter; no dark-moody next to bright-cheerful `[W:8ZX9y8GP0xs 2022, 3ahiFlIHvDc 2022]`.
- Interiors and fleets belong in services/lower sections so people see "exactly what they're walking into" `[W:7kG8otQJfws 2021]`.
- Budget: professional photography beats a pricier build `[W:RiNJ-rl4MSU 2022]`.
- Illustration can add personality where photos would be generic `[W:tIHQFcmYptw 2024]`.

## STRUCT-10 — Video on the site

- Types that earn their place: about/problem video (the main client problem, how you solve it, why you do it); video FAQs; how-the-process-works video; video testimonials and case studies; objection-busting videos in the FAQ `[W:K5is_yACb7M 2024, qvbLxQsic4w 2022]`.
- Mechanism: parasocial trust. Faces on screen are processed like real faces; people arrive at the consult "feeling like they know you". Talk straight to camera, be casual and authentic `[W:R7jjzeKgCYU 2023]`.
- Smartphone production is fine; make the setting look professional `[W:vTDe5SblNUk 2023]`.
- Make the player visible and center stage, not a tiny "play video" link `[W:Yd8HdycDiHI 2018]`; put the best line as a headline next to it for the people who won't press play `[W:mGbLhEY7nPE 2024]`.
- Engagement stats ("88% more time on site", "53× more likely to rank") are old recycled figures: illustrative `[W:vTDe5SblNUk 2023, 1kgolxbvCd8 ≤2024]`.

## STRUCT-11 — Animation and effects

Default: none. Distraction (dead clicks on decorative elements; a CTA competing with "15 other visually interesting things"), mobile breakage (hover effects don't exist on phones) and load time all cost conversions `[W:pRNny_t8TUE 2025]`. If used: subtle, consistent, lightweight, never on the CTA path (C9) `[W:vTDe5SblNUk 2023]`. No animated or arrow-laden CTA buttons ("scammy"); a strong color contrast is enough `[W:g_Wk6nJD1_Y 2025]`. No parallax.

## STRUCT-12 — Mobile

- Most traffic is mobile, "I've seen clients spend thousands on desktop effects that 70% of their visitors never even see" `[W:pRNny_t8TUE 2025]`. But check the device split in analytics before assuming: considered purchases (wedding planning) are often researched on desktop `[W:7hhQf3T3k-Q 2019]`.
- One hero image, stacked; bento tiles reorder or drop; video → static image when needed `[W:UMDUqWxstow 2025, tIHQFcmYptw 2024, 7hhQf3T3k-Q 2019]`.
- [G]: tap targets ≥ 44 px; a phone number as a `tel:` link; sticky bottom bar with the primary action for urgent trades (call) or booking; forms single-column with correct keyboard types (`cta-forms.md`) `[MS]`.
- [G]: the consent banner must not cover the hero CTA (`be-eu.md`).

## STRUCT-13 — Speed

People won't wait more than ~3 seconds, especially on mobile (Google: 53% of mobile visitors leave after 3 s; 1 s delay ≈ −7% conversions; both old, illustrative) `[W:kZdXTq5uOEI 2021, 3ahiFlIHvDc 2022, pRNny_t8TUE 2025]`. Heavy images, video and animation are the usual cause; optimize image sizes, audit GIFs, test with PageSpeed Insights `[W:3ahiFlIHvDc 2022, kZdXTq5uOEI 2021]`. [G] Targets (Core Web Vitals, 75th percentile of real users): LCP ≤ 2.5 s, INP ≤ 200 ms, CLS ≤ 0.1. Lazy-load below-the-fold media and third-party embeds (maps, video, booking widgets, review widgets) until interaction.

## STRUCT-14 — Content hub (optional)

A blog/vlog/podcast section as "bait" for ideal clients was the 2020 advice `[W:XMrQ-NEuZ7o 2020]`; by 2024–25 Wes says generic blog Q&A is answered by AI and "the only content that truly matters is video" `[W:db4tr3BAWJo 2024, QJnhnhXuuYM 2025]`. Keep Blog out of the main nav. If you publish, publish the "logistics answers" (price, comparisons, process, timelines, how to choose) described in `ai-search.md`; every content page needs its own relevant CTA `[W:cqh4JDnX6KU 2022]`.
