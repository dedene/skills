# 00 — Principles, evolution, contradictions

Knowledge base for four skills: `site-brief`, `conversion-audit`, `page-copy`, `lead-offer` (+ optional BE/EU layer). Scope: websites and landing pages for small and medium **service** businesses (trades, clinics, coaches, consultants, agencies, local pros). E-commerce catalogues are out of scope.

## The 15 principles, ranked

Ranked by leverage for a typical SMB service site with mostly cold or lukewarm traffic.

1. **One ideal customer, one site job, one primary action.** Write to one person, one offer, one promise, one CTA, one path. *Why:* every extra audience, offer or action splits attention; choice overload stalls a decision ("an overwhelmed mind makes no decision at all"). *When:* every page. Multi-service businesses keep the rule **per page** (one service landing page per offer). `[W:dKn4Zxpu9jk 2018, b0KQaZkDBKU 2018, 3ahiFlIHvDc 2022]`
2. **The hero must pass the 5-second test.** A stranger must answer: what do you do, why does it matter to me, what do I do next, and ideally why you. *Why:* visitors scan the hero like a billboard; if it confuses them they leave instead of scrolling for clarity ("confuse them here, lose them here"). Wes calls the hero "80% of the way there" (illustrative). *When:* always; the single highest-priority audit check. `[W:b0KQaZkDBKU 2018, 8ZX9y8GP0xs 2022, 2lYdI86YV30 2023, UMDUqWxstow 2025]`
3. **Clear beats clever, in the customer's own words.** Plain language at roughly a 7th–8th-grade reading level, lifted from how customers describe their problem. *Why:* decoding costs attention; mirrored language makes people feel understood ("this person gets me"). *Except:* clever lines survive only when the very next line resolves them. `[W:64ie_-xuMgQ 2021, aKUoWPGph-M 2024, rByridDOAKM 2025, IQtpeWeVqt0 2026]`
4. **The customer is the hero; the business is the guide.** Lead with their problem and the outcome they want. Introduce yourself later, as the mentor with empathy plus authority. *Why:* nobody cares about you yet; people want to see themselves on the page. *Except:* the About page, which is allowed to be about you (see contradiction C5). `[W:jPclFEiC2o4 2023, hDgjahAAi0g 2023, Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025, bWVyN9VoBB4 2026]`
5. **Sell the after-state, not the activity.** Headline, image and benefits show life after the problem is solved. *Why:* "you're almost never selling the thing that you do; you're selling the aftermath." *When:* hero image, headline, benefits, testimonials. `[W:OvQvDu5ZKzQ 2020, 7kG8otQJfws 2021, 64ie_-xuMgQ 2021, g_Wk6nJD1_Y 2025]`
6. **Follow a familiar layout and the proven section order; subtract before adding.** Use a prototypical service-business structure (hero → problem → guide → services → benefits → process → proof → features → FAQ → CTA). Be creative in color, type and imagery, never in navigation or structure. *Why:* familiar, simple layouts are judged more beautiful and credible and free working memory for your message; order works "like a combination lock". *When:* homepage and service pages. `[W:dvmMn1J-kqk 2023, jsOoglMSpdY 2024, Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025, pRNny_t8TUE 2025]`
7. **One specific CTA, repeated identically, in a color reserved for it.** Action verb plus what happens next ("Book a free 20-minute consultation"), the same text and style everywhere, a button color used nowhere else, and a reassurance line under it. *Why:* vague or inconsistent CTAs leave people unsure what happens next; repetition catches every "ready" moment. `[W:n5Sk2sz8E2Y 2022, 8ZX9y8GP0xs 2022, kZdXTq5uOEI 2021, mGbLhEY7nPE 2024]`
8. **Specific, human proof, placed next to the claim it proves.** Faces, names, result-headlined testimonials, unrounded numbers, objection-busting stories. *Why:* proof arriving at the moment of doubt resolves it; vague praise and stock faces make every claim look fake. `[W:RlMBBbXzQ84 2023, mGbLhEY7nPE 2024, K5is_yACb7M 2024, Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025]`
9. **Two paths: a primary CTA for the ready few, a transitional offer for everyone else.** Capture the not-ready majority (Wes's estimates range from 80–90% to 96%; illustrative) with a specific, useful lead magnet, then nurture by email. Never let the lead magnet replace the primary CTA. `[W:_hBEZT8-uOw 2018, jPclFEiC2o4 2023, g_Wk6nJD1_Y 2025, bWVyN9VoBB4 2026]`
10. **Remove friction after the click.** Self-booking beats "fill in a form and we'll call you"; forms ask the minimum; the button says exactly what happens. *Why:* uncertainty about the next step is itself a stopper. `[W:7hhQf3T3k-Q 2019, K5is_yACb7M 2024, dUrlwvm-CqQ 2024, ClD037qJSTQ 2025]`
11. **Talk about price.** Exact prices, ranges, "starting at", cost factors, example jobs or an estimator. *Why:* hidden prices breed doubt ("they'll charge the max"), send people to whoever answers first, and fill your calendar with unqualified calls. In 2026 Wes adds that AI assistants want "the closest thing to a real number". `[W:_XmzVYmVfJM 2024, ClD037qJSTQ 2025, VXGDHZIGf40 2026]`
12. **Be specific and differentiated.** A sharp positioning statement (what + for whom + result), a named signature method, specific claims instead of clichés, and a niche. *Why:* generic reads as commodity, so people shop on price. When AI builds the shortlist, the specialist gets named. `[W:RlMBBbXzQ84 2023, VxkGExMwZCc 2026, VXGDHZIGf40 2026]`
13. **Only truthful psychology.** Scarcity, social proof, anchors, "as seen on" and review claims must be real and verifiable. *Why:* fake signals convert once and then burn trust; in the EU several are also illegal (see `be-eu.md`). `[W:dKn4Zxpu9jk 2018, q_YbF26ji7w 2023, IQtpeWeVqt0 2026] [MS]`
14. **Fix message and structure before micro-tweaks.** Button color and field count matter less than a message that names the real feeling. Wes's guest Talia Wolf found element "hacks" rarely moved numbers; "it was mostly messaging". *When:* audit prioritisation. `[W:OtChoVhEI8Y 2025, mGbLhEY7nPE 2024] [MS]`
15. **Match the page to the traffic.** Paid clicks go to a dedicated offer page that matches the ad, never the homepage. Warm traffic (referrals, YouTube, AI recommendations) needs less persuasion and a faster path. Cold traffic needs the full argument. `[W:kZdXTq5uOEI 2021, WktIWzpeMis 2020, VXGDHZIGf40 2026] [MS]`

## Defaults when context is missing

Use these until the business context file says otherwise:

- Page = homepage; traffic = mixed cold organic + referrals; buyer = consumer or small-business owner; first step = free consultation or quote.
- Primary CTA = booking a free call or visit; transitional offer = one specific quick-win lead magnet.
- Structure = the 10-section blueprint (`page-structure.md`); hero = split layout, text left (`hero.md`).
- Tone = conversational "you", reading level grade 7–8 (US) ≈ CEFR B1 / "klare taal" (NL) / "langage clair" (FR), no jargon.
- Locale: if Belgian, apply `be-eu.md` (language, legal footer, consent, reviews law).

## How Wes's advice evolved, 2018 → 2026

| Period | Emphasis | Added | Dropped or reversed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2018–2019 | Psychology-trigger lists, landing-page "must-haves", homepage choice by offer mix | Rule of One; 5-second rule; 3 benefits + features checklist; ~60-second explainer video; testimonials with photos; exit-intent popup; "Proof" live-notification widget; publish prices even with custom quotes `[W:0c1Pu4Lc08M 2018]` | — |
| 2020–2021 | Hero makeovers, images, headline formulas, paid-ads landing pages, funnels on an owned site | 3 headline formulas; after-state hero image; ad-to-page visual continuity; CTA color ≠ brand/logo color; advertorial pre-sell page; blog as "bait" | Hero sliders tolerated as a workaround (dropped later) |
| 2022 | CTA doctrine hardens; design polish; lead-magnet diagnostics | Identical repeated CTA; "my" vs "your" test; nav ≤5; returning visitors (8×, illustrative); premium-look design; About = "not about you" formula | — |
| 2023 | Copy-first system and research-backed simplicity | "Content that sells, then design for it"; prototypicality (Google study); eye-tracking left rule; specificity and precision bias; "Customer Saga" order; Crestodina About framework; scarcity taxonomy; parasocial video | **About reversal:** "should fail the French test". **Dark backgrounds** now acceptable. |
| 2024 | Fixed-path homepage formula; voice-of-customer; pricing transparency; "remove" lists; landing-page micro-levers | IKEA 8-section order; VoC mining with AI; River Pools pricing case; 9 hero layouts; eyebrow copy; CTA booster line; TL;DR end panel; TL;DW video headline; Calendly over contact forms; remove social icons | **Text over background image** "generally not recommended anymore". Blog/SEO traffic declared shrinking; email + YouTube instead. |
| 2025 | The 10-section blueprint; storytelling; "good-enough design"; AI features | Hero proof + 3 outcome checkmarks; FAQ alternating objections; Talia Wolf storytelling (PAS, BAB, Hero's Journey); anti-animation and anti-parallax; AI chat, estimators, schedulers, custom-GPT magnets; AI images acceptable; one hero button | **Lottie/scroll animations** (recommended in 2023) now framed as conversion drag. **Secondary ghost button** (2024 layouts) deleted: "one is best". AI site builders "nowhere near ready" (April). |
| 2026 | Upstream positioning and YouTube; the website shrinks | Buyer fame; positioning statement; named signature method; storytelling per funnel stage; micro-apps instead of PDFs; logistics-answer content; "claim jumper" niche signals; AI citation logic | **Website role:** from "#1 salesperson" (2023) to "the last step… just don't get in the way" (Sept 2026). AI now "builds a great site in an hour or two" (reverses April 2025). Written copy "won't be the tiebreaker". |

**Stable across all eight years:** the three-part hero (what, why it matters, what next); client as hero; after-state imagery; clear over clever; one CTA; testimonials with faces; lead capture for non-buyers; truthful scarcity.

**Stats drift (illustrative):** "ready to buy now" moved from "10–20%" (2018) to "4%" (2023). "Seconds to judge" is variously 3, 5, 10 seconds and 50 ms. Treat all of them as rhetoric for "fast".

## Contradictions and how to resolve them

Each resolution is the rule the skills apply. "Open" means the sources don't settle it; the skills should ask or state the assumption.

| # | Conflict | Sources | Resolution rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **One hero button vs secondary CTA / ask ladder** | One button is best `[W:trfDoGoXNsE 2025]`; ghost secondary button in hero layouts `[W:tIHQFcmYptw 2024]`; vary ask sizes `[W:IQtpeWeVqt0 2026]`; primary/secondary hierarchy `[MS]` | Paid LP: one action, no secondary. Homepage: one filled primary button in the hero; the transitional offer lives as a text link or ghost button **below** it, or in its own lower section. Never two filled buttons. Never make the lead magnet the primary. **Partly open:** whether a hero text-link secondary helps is a test idea. |
| C2 | **Navigation** | Lean nav ≤5 + CTA `[W:3ahiFlIHvDc 2022, dUrlwvm-CqQ 2024]`; standalone offer pages "so people don't wander" `[W:kZdXTq5uOEI 2021]`; remove nav on LPs `[MS]` | Homepage and service pages: ≤5 nav items + CTA button top right. Paid landing pages: no nav, logo not linked or linked to home, minimal footer (legal only). **Hybrid** (an organic service page that also receives ad clicks): keep the nav, but no nav button that competes with the hero CTA; judge the hero's message match against the ads (A-M10); when ad spend is meaningful, recommend a dedicated paid landing page (STRUCT-03). |
| C3 | **Short vs long copy** | Cut homepage copy in half `[W:2ncy12qlabw 2023]`; About can be long, enlarge first sentence, bold key ideas `[W:w79jVvGlIDE 2023, att9LfaZBos 2023]` | Short where people decide (homepage, service, paid LP). Long is fine where people come to read (About, advertorial, detailed service/FAQ pages), made skimmable. |
| C4 | **Homepage story vs many pages vs one page** | Tell the whole story on the homepage `[W:8ZX9y8GP0xs 2022]`; you almost always need several pages `[W:ClD037qJSTQ 2025]`; one landing page can be enough `[W:VXGDHZIGf40 2026]`; separate page per service for ads `[W:b0KQaZkDBKU 2018]` | Homepage = a linear landing page with a taste of every section, each linking to a support page (services, pricing, testimonials, About, FAQ). Offer pages for ads. A single page is enough only when traffic arrives pre-sold (strong referrals, YouTube, AI). |
| C5 | **About page: about you or about them?** | "About isn't about you", no bios `[W:bYZkDOwQxfg 2022]`; "should fail the French test", be self-indulgent `[W:att9LfaZBos 2023]`; "about you in the context of how we help" `[W:7hhQf3T3k-Q 2019]` | About tells *your* story and values (Crestodina's 7 elements), but every "how we help" paragraph is framed as a client benefit, and it still ends in the CTA. No bio grids either way. Details in `page-types.md`. |
| C6 | **Team/founder on the homepage** | Team on About only `[W:7kG8otQJfws 2021]`; faceless = scammy, introduce yourself after the problem `[W:t4gF4aVRcDY 2024]`; first photo of you in the solution section `[W:Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025]`; never in the hero `[W:UMDUqWxstow 2025]` | Hero: customers, not you. Solution/guide section: one real photo of you or the team, a name, a city. About: group shot and story. **Except:** personal brands and known personalities may appear in the hero `[W:nfFlB0pmsWc 2020, g_Wk6nJD1_Y 2025]`. |
| C7 | **Headline: state what you do, or the result?** | "Headline = simply state what you do" `[W:8ZX9y8GP0xs 2022]`; headline = result, subhead = how `[W:w79jVvGlIDE 2023, Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025]`; three formulas `[W:64ie_-xuMgQ 2021]` | Choose by business type (HERO-02): transformation → result headline; problem-solving → problem headline; novel or utilitarian → plain what-you-do headline. The subhead always supplies the missing half. |
| C8 | **Images: real, stock or AI?** | "People hate obvious stock" `[W:_hBEZT8-uOw 2018]`; stock fine if natural `[W:ClD037qJSTQ 2025]`; AI images OK `[W:pRNny_t8TUE 2025]`; never AI/stock for the team `[W:trfDoGoXNsE 2025, dUrlwvm-CqQ 2024]` | Customer/mood images: good stock or AI if natural and on-avatar. Team, premises and portfolio work: real only. |
| C9 | **Animation and "delight"** | Lottie, hover, scroll animations add personality `[W:vTDe5SblNUk 2023]`; animations cause dead clicks, mobile breakage, slow loads `[W:pRNny_t8TUE 2025]` | Default: none. At most subtle micro-motion that never competes with the CTA, is lightweight, and degrades gracefully on mobile. |
| C10 | **Hero layout: text-left split vs text over media** | Text over background image not recommended `[W:tIHQFcmYptw 2024]`; split, text left `[W:UMDUqWxstow 2025]`; centered text on video in AI build demo `[W:trfDoGoXNsE 2025]` | Default split hero, text left. Overlay only on faceless images/video (spaces, groups, landscapes) with a dark overlay or a solid text box. |
| C11 | **Hero form vs hero button** | Hero form for urgent/utilitarian businesses, "I don't always recommend" `[W:tIHQFcmYptw 2024]` | Emergency and utility trades (locksmith, plumber, towing): click-to-call plus a 2–3 field callback form in the hero. Everyone else: a button. |
| C12 | **Contact form vs scheduler vs no email** | Short form + one emotional question `[W:7hhQf3T3k-Q 2019]`; remove the contact form, use Calendly `[W:dUrlwvm-CqQ 2024]`; forms let you ask qualifying questions, never publish your email `[W:ClD037qJSTQ 2025]` | Consult/appointment businesses: scheduler. Quote-based work: short quote form (or estimator). Urgent: phone. Never a vague "Contact us" form. EU law requires an email address in the legal info (`be-eu.md`); show it there, not as the main path. |
| C13 | **Price transparency vs gated calculators** | Publish prices, hiding breeds doubt `[W:_XmzVYmVfJM 2024]`; River Pools gates calculator results behind email, a lead engine `[W:_XmzVYmVfJM 2024, _2gU7G5U99I 2025]` | Publish ranges, "from" prices and cost factors ungated. Gate only the *personalized* detail (exact estimate, saved plan). **Open:** Wes never reconciles this; flag the trade-off to the owner. |
| C14 | **Live social-proof popups vs authenticity** | Recommends "X just booked" notifications (+10–15%, illustrative) `[W:_hBEZT8-uOw 2018, mGbLhEY7nPE 2024]`; fake scarcity backfires `[W:IQtpeWeVqt0 2026]`; manufactured FOMO `[MS]` | Only with real, current events, and never on premium or low-trust-industry sites. Static proof first. Treat as a test idea, not a default. |
| C15 | **"As seen on" via paid press releases** | Paid PR distribution to claim media logos `[W:rSWSXAFv1Gg 2021, mGbLhEY7nPE 2024]` | Do not recommend. Widely read as low-credibility, and possibly misleading to consumers `[G]`. Use earned mentions only. |
| C16 | **Open loops vs "leaving them hanging"** | Open loops keep viewers watching `[W:IQtpeWeVqt0 2026]`; unresolved headlines lose visitors `[W:64ie_-xuMgQ 2021]` | Loops belong in time-based media (video, email series). Web headlines resolve immediately. |
| C17 | **Section order variants** | 2021 redesigns: benefits right after hero; 2023 Saga: problem → solution → benefits → process → testimonials; 2024 IKEA: features last; 2025 blueprint: 10 sections | Canonical = 2025 blueprint. Allowed deviations in `page-structure.md` (e.g. emergency trades: benefits and proof before the problem, which is obvious). |
| C18 | **Is the website still the main salesperson?** | "#1 salesperson" `[W:2ncy12qlabw 2023]`; "last step, don't get in the way" `[W:VXGDHZIGf40 2026]` | Depends on the traffic source. Without a content engine or referrals, the site must still persuade cold visitors fully. With one, shorten the page and make booking faster. **Open:** Wes's 2026 claims are predictions with a commercial motive (he now sells YouTube training). |
| C19 | **"Words sell" vs "copy is commoditized"** | "The words sell your offers" `[W:ClD037qJSTQ 2025]`; AI makes every site well written `[W:VXGDHZIGf40 2026]` | Good copy is necessary but no longer enough to differentiate. The edge moves to real proof, specifics, a named method, niche signals and human presence (video, faces). |
| C20 | **Visual variety vs prototypicality** | Break up layouts to keep people scrolling `[W:Yd8HdycDiHI 2018]`; vary section layouts `[W:trfDoGoXNsE 2025]`; don't subvert layout expectations `[W:dvmMn1J-kqk 2023]` | Vary section styling (alternating image sides, one colored band) inside a conventional page order. Never invent novel navigation or reading order. |
| C21 | **First-person CTA** | "Start my free trial" beat "your" (+90%, illustrative) `[W:n5Sk2sz8E2Y 2022]`, yet his own examples use "your" | Test idea, not a rule. Default to whichever reads naturally in the language (NL/FR: see `be-eu.md`). |

## Things the sources don't cover (filled from [G]/[MS])
