# 25 — Page types: services, multiple services, About, testimonials, contact, FAQ, pricing

Site skeleton: "just a handful of key pages", only what's needed to get someone to book or join the list `[W:jPclFEiC2o4 2023]`. Homepage = the conversion engine for people close to deciding; other pages support those who need more before reaching out `[W:ClD037qJSTQ 2025]`.

Typical SMB service site `[W:3ahiFlIHvDc 2022, K5is_yACb7M 2024]` `[MS]`:

```
Home (landing page for the main offer)
├── Services (index, only if 2+ services) → one page per service
├── Pricing (or pricing inside each service page)
├── Reviews / Testimonials
├── About
├── FAQ (optional, can live on service pages)
├── Book / Contact (the CTA target)
└── Footer: blog/resources, jobs, client login, legal (privacy, cookies, terms), legal info
```

## Services page

**PAGE-01 — "Service pages are home pages."** Many visitors land there straight from Google; give each service page the same persuasive elements and order as the homepage instead of an SEO wall of text `[W:hDgjahAAi0g 2023]`.

Service page wireframe (blueprint condensed to one service) `[W:Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025, 2lYdI86YV30 2023, b0KQaZkDBKU 2018]`:

1. **Hero:** eyebrow (who it's for) · result headline for *this* service · subhead with what/how, format, timeline, service area · outcome checks · primary CTA + booster · proof (rating, one quote) · after-state image for this service
2. **Problem:** the symptoms specific to this service, in customer words
3. **Solution / how we do it:** your approach or named method in plain terms + one real photo (team on the job only if it shows the finished standard, not "the sausage being made" `[W:g_Wk6nJD1_Y 2025]`)
4. **Benefits:** 3, each paired with a testimonial proving it (`PROOF-07`)
5. **Process:** 3 steps, Step 1 = CTA wording
6. **What's included:** feature checklist (checkmarks), plus what's *not* included if that causes disputes
7. **Pricing:** price, range, "from", factors, or example jobs (`PRICE-03`); link to the estimator if any
8. **Comparison** (optional): vs DIY / typical provider / doing nothing
9. **FAQ:** 4–6, alternating real questions and objections-as-questions; objection videos if you have them `[W:qvbLxQsic4w 2022]`
10. **Final CTA** (same button) + transitional offer link for the not-ready
11. **SEO block** at the bottom if needed (location, variations), designed, not a text dump `[W:7kG8otQJfws 2021]`

Imagery: show the finished result or the happy client (computer repair: the happy customer using the fixed laptop, not the laptop in pieces) `[W:jPclFEiC2o4 2023]`. Interiors, fleet and equipment go lower as "see exactly what you're walking into" `[W:7kG8otQJfws 2021]`.

## Multiple services

**PAGE-02 — Pick a lane per page.** Homepage type by revenue mix (`STRUCT-01`) `[W:_hBEZT8-uOw 2018]`. On the homepage show 3–5 services (the ~80%-of-revenue ones) as cards linking to their own pages `[W:Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025]`; a long list in small type is never read `[W:7kG8otQJfws 2021]`.

**PAGE-03 — Add-ons stay add-ons.** Services a buyer wouldn't expect from the same vendor get their own nav tab or page and a light mention ("we've also got photo booths if you want one as an add-on"), never a shared headline `[W:7hhQf3T3k-Q 2019]`.

**PAGE-04 — One landing page per offer for ads and SEO.** Contractor with bathroom and kitchen remodels: separate pages, each with the full landing-page structure `[W:b0KQaZkDBKU 2018]`.

**PAGE-05 — Package paralysis → package finder.** Similar-sounding packages (brand strategy / marketing strategy / comprehensive) make people "freeze and not do anything". Fix: a short quiz that recommends the best-fit package, with the result gated behind email `[W:2lYdI86YV30 2023]`, plus a tier comparison table and a "Most popular" label (`PRICE-06`).

## About page

The About page is usually among the most-visited pages (often the 2nd; illustrative) and sits on the path to conversion `[W:bYZkDOwQxfg 2022, att9LfaZBos 2023]`. Wes has two formulas that point in different directions (C5). Resolution: **use the story framework for the "who and why"; use the sales wireframe's rules for how you describe your help.** Always end with the CTA.

**ABOUT-01 — Replace "About us" with a why-headline.** Templates `[W:bYZkDOwQxfg 2022]`:
- "I know firsthand how [problem] can sabotage [what they care about]" ("…how bad habits can sabotage your relationship with your dog")
- "We believe [belief]" ("…a healthy smile is the ultimate first impression")
- "You should [should-statement]" ("You should remember your wedding night as the most epic party of your life")
- Specific section heading style: "Why we're obsessed with keeping your pet healthy" `[W:RlMBBbXzQ84 2023]`

**ABOUT-02 — Tell the story with Crestodina's seven elements** `[W:att9LfaZBos 2023]`. The About page "is supposed to fail the French test": you may be self-indulgent, long, and go into values and why the company exists (Wes flags this as reversing his own earlier advice). People visit to feel a connection, find out who you are and why you started, confirm real humans exist, and connect with values (a 36,000-person survey: 58% choose businesses by values; illustrative).

| Element | What to include |
|---|---|
| Story | Beginning, middle, end, not a services list |
| Characters | Founders/team with real faces and names; the #1 mistake is an About page with no people that just repeats the homepage |
| Conflict | The "dragon": what was broken in the market that made the business necessary (stats + origin, a timeline triggered by a bad customer experience), or founder passion ("I wouldn't have been satisfied doing anything else") |
| Resolution | The outcome and impact: proof, and how you do more than others |
| Sequel | One line on where you're going next (most pages skip it) |
| Dialogue | Real quotes in quotation marks; video so words, face and tone align |
| Setting | Where you are: exterior photo, map, service area; "an indication of spam if you cannot tell where that business is" |

Format is flexible: video-heavy, a long story, or a single paragraph where each sentence ticks a box.

**ABOUT-03 — Describe your help as client benefits.** After a one-paragraph qualification story (first-hand experience, trial and error, training), write a few short paragraphs on how you help, each turning a brag into what it means for them (the DJ dance-floor rewrite) `[W:bYZkDOwQxfg 2022]`. "About you in the context of how we help our customers" `[W:7hhQf3T3k-Q 2019]`. Solo businesses: a personal why-story (the market problem you had to solve, or why you can't picture doing anything else); "people connect with stories and passion, not credentials" `[W:ClD037qJSTQ 2025]`.

**ABOUT-04 — Proof on About.** A testimonial supporting the specific claims just made (not "great to work with") and/or a stats section (clients, reviews, years, money or time saved) `[W:bYZkDOwQxfg 2022]`.

**ABOUT-05 — Real people, no bio grids.** Real photos or video of you/the team; never stock or AI for the team ("leaves a bad taste", "feels like hiding") `[W:bYZkDOwQxfg 2022, dUrlwvm-CqQ 2024, trfDoGoXNsE 2025]`. No team-bio grids with hobbies, pizza toppings or links to each member's socials: they distract, send people off the path to purchase, and exist for team morale, not clients. One friendly group shot is enough `[W:bYZkDOwQxfg 2022, dUrlwvm-CqQ 2024, 7hhQf3T3k-Q 2019]`.

**ABOUT-06 — Credentials last.** Awards, qualifications and press go near the end; essential for doctors, lawyers and analytical buyers, but "the point is just not to lead with that stuff" `[W:bYZkDOwQxfg 2022]`. Mention them, "just make a little less of a deal about them" `[W:ClD037qJSTQ 2025]`.

**ABOUT-07 — About video.** The main client problem, how you solve it, why you do it `[W:K5is_yACb7M 2024]`. Talking to camera builds the parasocial "I feel like I know you already" effect `[W:R7jjzeKgCYU 2023]`.

About wireframe:
1. Why-headline (ABOUT-01) + a real photo
2. The story: conflict/dragon → why you started (1–3 short paragraphs, enlarged first sentence)
3. How we help (benefits, not brags) + the named method, if any
4. Proof (testimonial matching the claims, stats row)
5. Values (specific, not "quality and integrity") + dialogue (quotes/video)
6. Setting (map, area, premises) + credentials
7. Sequel (one line) + CTA

## Testimonials / reviews page

From the 2018 page teardowns plus later rules `[W:Yd8HdycDiHI 2018, K5is_yACb7M 2024]`:

- Hero: large image of smiling customer faces (don't lay text over faces; no busy backgrounds), a big pull quote, an identification headline ("They did it, so can you").
- Visible, center-stage video player; a pull-quote summary next to every video.
- Story-driven video testimonials with B-roll; interleaving several customers' stories works well.
- Unified look for all cards and videos (same framing and styling).
- Cards: photo + benefit-selling quote as headline + "read the full story" link; the headline must carry the proof because most won't click.
- Long stories standardized into skimmable parts (how it started / how it helped / advice for others), short paragraphs.
- Segment labels or filters when you serve several personas, plus "see more like this".
- Break up long lists by switching layouts (list → 3 columns → featured colored block) inside a conventional page.
- Show volume ("this company has a lot of fans"): many cards, review count, rating.
- Link out to the full Google reviews profile `[W:7kG8otQJfws 2021]`.
- End with the primary CTA.

## Contact / booking page

Mostly [G], built on Wes's rules:

- Title it after the action ("Book your free consultation"), not "Contact".
- Primary: the scheduler embed or the short form (`cta-forms.md`); state duration, format, cost and response time.
- Secondary: phone (`tel:` link), opening hours, service area, map (setting `[W:att9LfaZBos 2023]`), WhatsApp if you actually answer it [G].
- One line of proof (rating, short quote) next to the form `[MS]`.
- No bare email as the main path `[W:ClD037qJSTQ 2025]`; legal email in the legal info (`be-eu.md`).
- Client login lives here or in the footer, never top-right `[W:7hhQf3T3k-Q 2019]`.

## FAQ page

- On the homepage ~6, on the FAQ page more; alternate real questions with objections rephrased as questions and answered with your best rebuttal: "a smart almost-closer" `[W:Zxz-0r8G7KQ 2025]`.
- Record short objection videos: list the top objections, write a rebuttal for each, open with "I know my clients, and many of them told me they were worried about [X], but here's the truth…" `[W:qvbLxQsic4w 2022]`. Widespread objections get their own homepage section `[W:qvbLxQsic4w 2022, hDgjahAAi0g 2023]`.
- Include the logistics answers (price, process, timelines, comparisons, how to choose) that AI assistants and buyers look for `[W:VXGDHZIGf40 2026]`.
- FAQ video pages: question as headline, video + text answer, same question as the video title `[W:1kgolxbvCd8 ≤2024]`.
